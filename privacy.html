Privacy & website analytics

Updated November 27, 2026.

Project requests and applications

When you submit a form, FormSubmit processes the details and attachments you choose to provide and emails them to WeaversHBC@gmail.com. We use those details to respond to your project or employment inquiry. Please send only information needed for that inquiry. FormSubmit uses its own confirmation and spam protection services.

Optional website analytics

We ask before loading Google Analytics on this site. If you allow analytics, Google receives website usage information such as pages visited, referral sources, device information, and clicks on our phone links. Cookies may be stored on your browser. We do not send the contents of forms, names, email addresses, phone numbers entered into forms, or uploaded files as analytics events.

A phone-link click measures a tap, not a completed call. A form confirmation measures a return from the form provider, not verified email delivery or a booked job. Analytics excludes visitors who decline or block it, so its counts will be incomplete.

You can change your choice using Analytics Settings in the footer. Declining leaves the website and forms available. Allowing analytics starts collection from that point; changing your choice does not remove information already collected by Google.
